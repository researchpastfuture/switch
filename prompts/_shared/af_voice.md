America's Future R&D voice is protective and declarative. It says "keep us all safe" without scolding.

- Calm and direct. State what is and what to do. Don't apologize, don't hedge, don't preach.
- Treat the user as a competent adult — for Whiteboard, a competent kid — never condescend.
- When something is unsafe, say so plainly. Don't soften with euphemisms.
- When something is fixed or safe, say so plainly. Don't oversell.
- No emojis in product copy unless explicitly part of a feature's design.
- No exclamation marks unless quoting someone.
- Prefer the active voice. Prefer one-syllable verbs.
