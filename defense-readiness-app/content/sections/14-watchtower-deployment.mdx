## Overview

This section lays out the document's **phased deployment schedule** for the watchtower model (introduced in Section 4) and its proposed roadmap for expansion through the year 2030.

### A phased approach

Rather than proposing immediate full-scale implementation, the authors describe a deliberate, staged rollout. The argument is that capacity should be built and tested in stages — starting small, learning from pilots, and expanding only as the approach proves workable. The roadmap moves outward over time, from national pilots to regional coverage and eventually to coordination with allied partners.

The interactive **Deployment & Expansion Timeline** presents the milestones and lets readers filter by region (National, Regional, or Global). The phases the document outlines are:

- **Phase 1 — 2026 (National):** Pilot watchtower nodes — stand up initial nodes and training.
- **Phase 2 — 2027 (Regional):** Regional expansion — extend coverage to regional hubs.
- **Phase 3 — 2028 (National):** Civic brigade integration — integrate school shields and brigades.
- **Phase 4 — 2029 (Global):** Allied coordination — coordinate with allied partners.
- **Phase 5 — 2030 (Global):** Full deployment — reach target deployment and benchmarks.

These dates and phases are the document's proposed schedule, offered for planning and discussion rather than as fixed commitments.

### What the sequence reveals

The ordering reflects the document's priorities. **Training precedes expansion** — Phase 1 emphasizes standing up nodes *and* training before scaling. **Integration comes mid-roadmap** — Phase 3 folds in the school shields and civic brigades from Section 7, indicating that the authors see the community layer as central rather than peripheral. **Global coordination comes last** and is framed in terms of working with allied partners, consistent with the document's lawful, cooperative posture.

### Connections within the document

This timeline operationalizes the watchtower concept from Section 4 and connects directly to the metrics dashboard in Section 15: the final phase is defined in terms of reaching target benchmarks, so progress along the timeline is meant to be judged against measurable results rather than assumed from the calendar. As with the rest of the document, the schedule is presented as the authors' plan — a structured intention that readers can examine, question, and adjust.
