## Overview

This section presents the document's **restoration metrics and deterrence benchmarks** — its proposed way of measuring whether the doctrine is working. The authors argue that goals without measurement tend to drift, and that accountability requires tracking concrete indicators over time.

### Measuring progress, not just intentions

The premise of this section is that each major proposal in the document should produce results that can be observed and counted. By defining a baseline (the starting point), a current value, and a target, the authors create a simple way to see how far an effort has come and how far it has to go.

The interactive **Restoration Metrics Dashboard** shows each indicator as a progress bar, where completion is calculated as *(current − baseline) ÷ (target − baseline)*. The indicators the document tracks are:

- **Schools with civic defense brigades** — baseline 0, current 1,200, target 50,000.
- **Active watchtower nodes** — baseline 0, current 35, target 500.
- **Documented rescues / recoveries** — baseline 0, current 480, target 10,000.
- **Families completing preparedness program** — baseline 0, current 9,500, target 250,000.
- **Strategic stockpile coverage** — baseline 5%, current 22%, target 90%.

These figures are illustrative values the document offers to demonstrate the framework; they are editable and are not presented as verified official statistics.

### How the metrics tie the document together

Each indicator maps to an earlier section, which is the point. **Civic defense brigades** trace to Section 7, **watchtower nodes** to Sections 4 and 14, **rescues and recoveries** to Section 8, **families completing preparedness** to Section 3's self-assessment, and **stockpile coverage** to Section 6. The dashboard is, in effect, a single scoreboard for the whole doctrine.

### What the document emphasizes

The authors use this section to reinforce a theme that runs through the funding and legislative chapters: **commitments should be accountable.** Targets make priorities explicit, the gap between current and target makes honest reporting possible, and tracking over time guards against complacency. The metrics also connect to the deployment timeline (Section 14), whose final phase is defined as reaching these benchmarks.

As elsewhere, the dashboard is offered as a framework for thinking and discussion. Readers are encouraged to treat the numbers as starting points, to ask how each would be measured in practice, and to replace them with verified figures where available.
