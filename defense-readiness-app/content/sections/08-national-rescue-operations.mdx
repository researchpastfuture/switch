## Overview

This section presents the document's **rescue-operations and sanctuary-entry protocol** — an ordered, step-by-step process for responding to reports of exploitation or endangerment in a lawful, coordinated, and trauma-informed way.

### A protocol, not a free hand

The most important feature of this section, as the authors present it, is that response is **procedural and authorized at every step**. The protocol is explicitly built around coordination with law enforcement and authorized partners; it does not describe or endorse independent action. Its purpose is to ensure that good intentions translate into safe, lawful outcomes for everyone involved.

The interactive **Rescue & Sanctuary Entry Protocol** stepper lets a reader move through the stages and check each one off, with a progress indicator. The steps are:

1. **Intake & verification** — confirm the report and document the situation through proper channels.
2. **Coordinate with authorities** — engage law enforcement and authorized partners *before* any action.
3. **Assess safety & risk** — evaluate risk to the individual and to responders.
4. **Secure sanctuary placement** — identify a vetted, lawful safe placement.
5. **Transport under protocol** — move only with authorized personnel and proper documentation.
6. **Stabilize & provide care** — provide medical, trauma-informed, and basic-needs support.
7. **Follow-up & casework** — continue casework, recovery resources, and review.

### What the document emphasizes

Several principles run through the protocol. **Verification comes first**, so that response is grounded in confirmed information. **Authorities are engaged early**, reflecting the document's consistent insistence that rescue work belongs in cooperation with law enforcement and the proper agencies. **Care continues after the immediate response**, with follow-up and casework treated as part of the operation rather than an afterthought.

The authors frame this section as the point where the document's awareness functions connect to lawful action. Concerns surfaced through community vigilance or the watchtower model (Section 4) do not lead to improvised intervention; they enter a defined, accountable process designed to protect the vulnerable person, the responders, and the integrity of any subsequent legal proceedings.

### A note on scope

This edition presents the protocol as a framework for understanding and discussion. It is intentionally high-level and procedural; it is not operational guidance, and it does not substitute for professional training, legal counsel, or the direction of qualified authorities. Anyone facing a real situation is directed by the document's own logic to contact law enforcement and appropriate professionals first.
